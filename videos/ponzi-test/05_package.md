## Description

```
The original Ponzi scheme: in 1920, Charles Ponzi promised Boston fifty percent in ninety days, and thousands lined up to hand him their savings.

It started with an international reply coupon in a letter from Spain and $150. By June 1920, 7,824 people had paid in about $2.5 million in a single month, and his clerks were stacking cash in closets to the ceiling. Then a newspaper started asking questions, and in three days $1.7 million left his bank account.

This is the story of the man whose name became the word for this kind of fraud: the run on his notes, the guilty plea, the investors who got back cents on the dollar, and the warning signs that were there from the start.
```
