# Fact-check: ponzi-test

The script is accurate. 26 of 27 claims are backed by the dossier, mostly by court records (the Supreme Court and First Circuit opinions in [1], [4] and [2]). Every number, date and quote checks out. The one weak claim is that postal inspectors found coupon sales far too small: it rests only on a search excerpt of the Postal Museum page, so I've suggested crediting the point to Zuckoff instead. Legal risk is low. The script describes Ponzi's guilty plea and conviction precisely, he died in 1949, and the living-era figures (McMasters, the judge-lawyer) are not accused of anything. The script also correctly leaves out the doubtful details (the $15 million figure, the 'Pilgrims' quote, Judge Hale, the $61 in coupons).
